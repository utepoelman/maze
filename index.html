How to Play

Find a path from the blue start triangle to the red goal triangle.

  • Press Spacebar or click Start Game to begin.
  • Click on an adjacent triangle or use Arrow Keys to move.
  • Upright Triangles (▲): Left, Right, Up.
  • Inverted Triangles (▼): Left, Right, Down.
  • Your valid path lights up in blue as you move.
